# The cast of Coco Spark TV

Agreed between Claude and GPT on 2026-08-19. Names are WORKING NAMES — Pavan has the final
say, because a name becomes a sung, searched and spoken brand asset and neither of us should
lock one in a design message. Visual identity first.

## The shape of the show

**A South Asian human family at the centre, with the animals we already own as the second
ring.** The family carries everyday routines — teeth, bath, food, sleep, doctor, school —
which are the highest-volume preschool searches and only read as a family. Coco, Nana Willow
and Pip carry the imaginative layer and give the channel an identity that is not a weaker
copy of a human-family show.

The single most under-used fact in this project is in `mini/README.md`, recorded as a design
constraint: **the audience is 48% India and 14% Bangladesh.** Every episode we had designed
until now could have been made by anyone, anywhere. The family is explicitly South Asian —
ambiguous means American by default, and 62% of this audience already has that.

**But South Asian is not a costume.** Everyday episodes use contemporary children's and
family clothing. Sarees, kurtas, festival clothes, school uniforms and pyjamas are
*contextual wardrobe sets*, exactly as CoComelon puts JJ in swim trunks at the beach and
dungarees on the farm. What stays fixed is identity: face, hair, skin tone and a signature
colour. Permanently encoding "grandmother = saree" would repeat the fixed-outfit mistake the
format research already caught.

makes a statement we did not intend to make.

## Designed for 3D reconstruction, not for beautiful 2D art

GPT's constraint, and it is the most valuable line in the whole discussion. A single image
cannot recover what a mesh needs, and we would only find out after modelling:

- large head, preschool proportions, short limbs
- clean simple facial features, no fine detail around eyes or mouth
- **simple mitten-like hands**, not five articulated fingers
- **hair as a solid shape**, not strands, symmetrical enough to exist from behind
- no glasses, jewellery, badges, buttons, laces or fabric patterns
- an unmistakable silhouette — blacked out, it is still obviously this character
- one stylisation language shared with Coco, so they belong in the same show

## The order, and why

**Aadi first, alone.** Both of us inspect the actual pixels, revise until Pavan says "that is
our child", and only then derive the rest of the family from that visual language.
Generating four sheets at once would independently invent four attractive people who do not
look related or even from the same show.

And before anything is modelled: **Aadi and Coco side by side at thumbnail size.** If they
read as characters from two different franchises, the art direction is wrong and no amount of
modelling fixes it.

Then Aadi runs the 3D gates — identity, rig-check, one static Blender frame, performance —
before the rest of the family is generated. Scaling something proven beats multiplying an
unanswered question.
