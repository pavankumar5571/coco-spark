# ADR: Coco Spark TV is an animal-led IP

Decided 2026-08-19 by Pavan, GPT and Claude jointly. Supersedes the human-family cast
direction in adeedca.

## Decision

Coco Spark TV is an ORIGINAL ANIMAL-LED preschool learning/adventure IP.

  launch cast   Coco + Pip + Nana Willow   (already owned, already canonical)
  humans        none permanent; incidental only, if ever
  new characters only when a story role proves REPEATEDLY necessary
  premise       curiosity triggers a Spark; ordinary things become learning adventures

Zero new character sheets before the 3D gate.

## Why

The research split the market in a way that decided it.

Three of the largest preschool channels — Ms Rachel, Blippi, Kids Diana — are REAL PEOPLE
ON CAMERA. That format is structurally closed to us and should never have been treated as
a model.

Of animated properties: the subscriber leader is a human family (CoComelon, ~200M subs,
3rd most-subscribed channel on YouTube). The properties that became global IP in 180+
countries with durable licensing are both animal-led (Peppa Pig, Bluey).

The deciding argument: AN ANIMAL CAST HAS NO ETHNICITY. Pavan's requirement is global, not
any particular audience. A human family forces a permanent ethnicity decision — one GPT
correctly refused to make from traffic data, since character identity is expensive to
change and audience geography is not. An animal cast makes that decision unnecessary
rather than difficult. That is why Peppa and Bluey ship worldwide without redesigning
anyone.

Production reality reinforced it. We already own three canonical animal identities that
hold through the pipeline — proven, not hoped. Human faces, hands and anatomy are the
hardest thing for this stack and the fastest route to uncanny, and every human sheet
forces the ethnicity choice again on every character, forever.

## What we explicitly did NOT conclude

Animals do not CAUSE licensing success. GPT's correction, taken: Peppa and Bluey reflect
writing, character design, distribution and brand execution. Their reach is evidence that
animal characters CAN travel, not proof that choosing animals guarantees anything.

Nor is the lane empty. The saturation problem is not "too many animals" — it is too many
interchangeable animal worlds with no distinct engine. B only works if Coco Spark has its
own premise rather than animal versions of familiar routines.

## Rejected

A. HUMAN FAMILY + animal ring. The most crowded positioning in preschool, invites direct
   comparison to a 200M-subscriber incumbent, forces a permanent ethnicity decision, is
   hardest for our pipeline, and needs four new human identities before anything ships.
   Also: building a human family around a channel called Coco Spark, with Coco as the
   sidekick, is the weaker version of what we already have.

B'. CHILDREN + ANIMALS HYBRID. Diffusing ethnicity across a multicultural group is not
   deciding it — every child still has a specific skin tone, hair and face. Doubles the
   identity surface and has the hardest QC of the three.

## Also rejected: an animal HOUSEHOLD

Do not create a family template with animals in it. That drifts toward the structure we
just rejected. Start with the three characters we own and let recurring characters emerge
from the format.

## Consequence for sequencing

This costs nothing to adopt and unblocks immediately. E01 is already an animal-led bedtime
episode with an accepted 12 seconds. Next gate is technical, not creative:

  Coco turnaround -> Tripo 3D -> inspect front/three-quarter/side/back identity

If Coco survives 3D, do Pip and Nana Willow. If Coco fails, we learned it before creating
five more characters or spending a month on the wrong production method.
