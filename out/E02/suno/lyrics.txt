[Verse 1]
Five little stars are shining bright,
Peeking through my window light.
Coco counts them, one by one,
Softly now the day is done.

[Chorus]
Twinkle, twinkle, close your eyes,
Sleepy stars in sleepy skies.
Count them with me, drift away,
Goodnight, goodnight, till a brand new day.

[Verse 2]
Four little stars, then three, then two,
One last star is watching you.
Coco yawns and snuggles deep,
Warm and cosy, off to sleep.

[Outro]
Goodnight, goodnight, little bear,
Goodnight, goodnight, everywhere.
