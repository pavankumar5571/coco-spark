# E02 — before a single Suno credit moves

1. CONFIRM THE SUBSCRIPTION TIER. Free-tier Suno output is not licensed for
   commercial use. This episode is intended for a monetised channel. If the tier
   is not one that grants commercial rights, STOP — nothing else here matters.
2. Read lyrics.txt as a parent, not as an engineer. It is original work and it is
   the first thing anyone will hear from this channel.
3. Generate with the CLI from the enterprise-ai-yt implementation, CAPTCHA
   automation disabled, and pull the word timings — the timings are the point.
   The picture is cut to the song, not the other way around.
4. Bring the track back and run `make.py beatmap E02`.

No Gemini image or video call is authorised before the timings exist. Beats are
chosen by where the phrases fall, and buying pictures first would mean buying
pictures for cuts nobody has heard yet.
