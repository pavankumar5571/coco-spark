## Before uploading — the two things arithmetic cannot do

1. WATCH it end to end. Not the frames, the film.
2. LISTEN to it on headphones AND on a speaker. The mix measures -14.0 LUFS, which says nothing about whether the chord is pleasant, irritating, ominous or simply amateur. If it sounds synthetic and cheap, say so — the bed is a prototype, not a decision.

## At upload

- Visibility: UNLISTED first. Public is a separate, later decision.
- Audience: YES, made for kids. This removes comments, notifications, cards, end screens and personalised ads — do not design around any of them.
- Altered or synthetic content: decide per metadata.json; the honest answer for stylised animation with no real people is probably no, but it is your call.
- Title, description and tags: metadata.json, edit freely.

## After it processes

- Play it back ON YouTube, on phone and on a TV if you can.
- Check the volume against another kids' video. YouTube normalises loud material on playback; ours is quiet and should not be touched.
- Note every defect the platform introduced or revealed, and bring them back — that list is the only reason this upload exists.
